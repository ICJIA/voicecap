Reading transcripts

Each page gets a folder, with read.txt, headings.txt, and tab.txt in it.

Each line of a transcript is what the screen reader said after one key press.

An example

This page's headings.txt starts like this:

main landmark, Reading transcripts, heading, level 1
An example, heading, level 2

What a line tells you

A heading gives its text and its level, and a link says that it's a link.

A form field gives its label, if it has one, and what kind of field it is.

This demo site comes with voicecap, and runs only on this computer.
