The report

After the last page, voicecap builds a report, report.html, about every page.

Coverage

For each page, the report shows what each pass captured, and where it stopped.

Flags

Flags point to pages worth a closer listen, such as a field with no name.

A flag never fails a page: a person listens, and decides.

Reviews

A reviewer records what they found, and voicecap keeps every review.

This demo site comes with voicecap, for trying it out.
