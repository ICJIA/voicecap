# How voicecap checks NVDA's log against the transcripts

The full rules behind [NVDA's own log, checked against the transcripts](../README.md#the-shareable-page), on the shareable page.

## How a step is paired with NVDA's log

Each NVDA session is checked with its own copy, which the run's event log names (the `screen-reader-log` event after the session's stop), never by the copy's number, so one session without a copy can't shift the others onto the wrong log. A pass is checked only inside the window of the attempt that was kept for its page, from that attempt's start to its finish in the event log, to the millisecond. The keys and speech of any other attempt (a failed one, or one the person stopped) and of any other page are never paired with a step, and what falls between pages is outside the steps. The first Tab on each page goes to the browser, not through NVDA, so NVDA's log has no key for that step, and the check finds its speech by when it was said, at the moment the pass's other keys give it. Every other key goes to the step in whose time it was pressed: voicecap presses a step's key once NVDA is quiet, so the key can come seconds into its step when NVDA was still talking as the step began (about a map in a frame, say). Keys come in their steps' order, so a key NVDA took late, after its step's time, still goes to its step where order says whose it is: when it falls between the keys of the steps around it, or when a later step's time holds it, that step's own key, and a key for each step between them, as when NVDA took several keys together. When two ways of pairing a pass's keys with its steps account for as many keys, as when a key no step pressed comes just before the pass's first step or after its last, or NVDA didn't log the key of its first or last step, or when a key is left over among the pass's keys, which order couldn't place, as when a key no step pressed comes among them or NVDA logged one twice, the pass can't be paired for certain, and its steps aren't checked. Nor are they when NVDA's log has nothing at the moment the pass's other keys give its first Tab, since they may give it the wrong one. Two things wrong at once can still mislead the pairing, as no real run has shown. The cases found include a step whose key NVDA didn't log, or two keys in a row taken late, with a key no step pressed, or one NVDA logged twice, elsewhere in the pass; two keys in a row taken late, twice in a pass; and keys taken late with a step whose key NVDA didn't log. The steps between them can then each be paired with a neighbor's key, one step off. They're listed where the words they're given differ from their lines, but a real gap among them can go unshown: a step agrees where neighboring lines read the same (headings that all say "News"), and a step left without a key, whose words came in a later step's time, counts as having none. The check never looks at what was said to decide which speech is a step's.

## When a step agrees

Every letter and digit is the same, in the same order. NVDA's log holds the text NVDA was given, and a transcript holds what NVDA said once it had turned symbols into words ("read.txt" is said "read dot txt"), so where the log has a symbol, the transcript can have the symbol, nothing, or up to four words for it, and the check allows that. The check doesn't know NVDA's names for symbols, so those words aren't compared with them: any one to four can stand for a symbol, and "Up 5%" agrees with "Up 5 percent", as NVDA says it, and with "Up 5 is not good" alike. The one exception is a closing mark (a full stop, comma, semicolon, colon, exclamation or question mark, or an ellipsis) right after a letter or digit at the end of an item: NVDA doesn't say these at its usual symbol level, so one can only be there or not, and words after a line's last full stop are always a difference. A closing mark that follows another mark isn't right after a letter or digit, so it can stand for words too: "Really?!" agrees with "Really now please". A step too long to compare this way (about 2,000 characters on each side) agrees only when its words are the same, spaces aside (NVDA says "R3" as "R 3"). On the demo's seven pages, as NVDA read them on 6 October 2026, all 204 steps agree, and 177 lines NVDA spoke outside the steps aren't compared.

## Steps that weren't checked

A step isn't checked when the page can't pair it with a copy: voicecap kept no copy for its NVDA session (the run's own reason is given when it has one), the copy isn't as the run recorded it, the run's record doesn't list the copy its event log names, the copy has no speech in it (as when NVDA's logging level is below Input/output), the run kept only the first thing NVDA said for each step (`capture` set to `"initial"`), the times of the pages couldn't be read, the event log doesn't show when a page was read, the transcripts' steps couldn't be read here, or the keys in the copy couldn't be paired with a pass's steps for certain (see "How a step is paired with NVDA's log"). Those steps are counted, with the reason, and never listed as differences. A run whose screen reader isn't NVDA says the check is NVDA's only.
