main landmark, heading, level 1, Behind the Founding of i 2i
In the diverse landscape of the State of Illinois, thousands of organizations diligently engage
with communities, striving to make a difference. However, the struggle with capacity remains a
common challenge, exacerbated by historical disinvestment that has left many grassroots
organizations vulnerable and at risk of dissolution.
These organizations face fundamental challenges and organizational needs that demand urgent
attention. i 2i emerged as a response to this pressing issue, aiming to be a transformative force.
Its inception acknowledges the precarious position of grassroots organizations and their essential
role in community well-being amid historical disinvestments.
Historical disinvestment has cast a shadow over the resilience of grassroots organizations, and i 2i
stands committed to addressing these challenges. Firm in the belief that with the right resources
and assistance, these grassroots organizations can not only weather the storm but thrive.
i 2i focuses on grassroots organizations dedicated to changing the circumstances of violence in
their communities. By providing essential support, i 2i becomes a resource to ensure that their
capacity is not merely sustained but elevated, guiding them on a path to sustainability.
Together, we shape a future where capacity is not a barrier but a steppingstone to sustained impact.

Capacity building is the intentional process of, developing and strengthening the skills, systems,
processes, and resources, an organization needs to operate effectively and sustainably.
i 2i is dedicated to providing support to grassroots organizations and community groups by
