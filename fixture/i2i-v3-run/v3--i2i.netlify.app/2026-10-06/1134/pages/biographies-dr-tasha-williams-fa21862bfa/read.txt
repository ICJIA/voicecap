Dr. Tasha Williams has served as an Assistant Professor of Business, Dean of Business Programs, and
Dean of Juvenile Correctional Programs at various universities and colleges in Illinois. Dr. Tasha
has also served as an Executive Director and Capacity Building Program and Fiscal Manager for
community organizations around the State of Illinois. Dr. Tasha earned her bachelor’s degree from
the University of Missouri St. Louis in 2007, her master’s in business administration (M dot B dot A) with
a concentration in finance from Colorado Technical University in 2009, and her Doctorate in
Education, with a focus on Interdisciplinary Leadership and Higher Education, from Governors State
University in 2017. Dr. Tasha has developed and implemented mentoring programs for youth serving
nonprofit organizations around the State of Illinois. She founded a nonprofit organization called
Save One Life Foundation, which works with reentry youth to provide opportunities for them to
become productive members of society.
